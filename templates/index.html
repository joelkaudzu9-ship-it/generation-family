Our Rhythms

These aren't events. They're the heartbeat of who we are.

Prayer Gatherings

We meet multiple times a week to pray — on campus, in homes, and wherever two or three are gathered.

Fasting Days

Regular days of corporate fasting. Not obligation. Devotion. The whole family pressing in together.

Family Meals

Small groups that share food, share life, and share what they have. This is where strangers become family.

Retreats

Every midsemester break, we withdraw together. Fully funded by the generous giving of members.

Our Story

In 2019, two students at Kamuzu University of Health Sciences started praying together. No building. No budget. No name. Just two people and a conviction that their generation was destined for more.

That prayer grew into a community. That community grew into a movement. Today, The Generation Family spans {{ settings.get('cities', '5') }} cities, hosts retreats every midsemester break, runs family groups that share meals and resources, and operates a marketplace where members buy from one another.

The founders have graduated. The movement hasn't stopped.
