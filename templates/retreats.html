Retreats

Every midsemester break, we withdraw together

Twice a year, during the university midsemester breaks, The Generation Family leaves campus and city life behind. We go away together — to pray, to rest, to reconnect, and to encounter God away from the noise.

These retreats are entirely funded by the generous, joyful giving of members. Nobody is turned away for lack of funds. Everyone who wants to come, comes.
