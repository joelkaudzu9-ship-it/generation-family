Foundational Principles

1. Biblical Authority

All leadership decisions at every level must be grounded in Scripture. God's Word remains the ultimate authority over the movement.

2. Vision Above Individual Interests

The vision of The Generation takes precedence over personal preferences, departmental agendas, or group interests.

3. Independent Advisory Board

The actions, conduct, and performance of the Senior Leadership Team are guided by an Independent Advisory Board. Areas of accountability include theological accuracy, abuse of any kind, and progress toward organizational goals. Board membership and contact information are published on this website.

4. Ministry Heads Above Technical Teams

Ministry Heads serve as pastoral and leadership overseers, offering spiritual direction, mentorship, and governance to their technical teams.

5. Two Membership Categories

Foundation School Graduates (FSG Members) and Affiliate Members. Only FSG Members are eligible for appointments into leadership roles across the organization.
