Marketplace

A community economy — members buy from members

The marketplace is one of the most distinctive things about The Generation Family. It's a simple idea: before you buy from a stranger, check if someone in the family can provide it. Skills, goods, and services circulate within the community. Everyone benefits. Everyone belongs.

Goods

Clothing, household items, books, and more — bought and sold within the family.

Skills & Services

Tutoring, design, tailoring, catering, transport — members serving members.

Produce & Food

Homegrown produce, baked goods, and prepared meals shared within the community.
